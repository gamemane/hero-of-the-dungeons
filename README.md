Hero of the Dungeons — a classic top-down roguelike dungeon crawler, built to be deep, replayable, and completely free.

Descend into an ever-changing dungeon: every floor is procedurally generated with 80×60 mazes of rooms, winding corridors and hidden secrets. Fight 7 enemy types, dodge spike traps, and face the Dungeon Lord himself on every 5th floor.

Build your hero your way. There are no fixed classes — you craft your own playstyle with 28 talents across 5 archetype builds: a tanky Warrior, a spell-slinging Mage, a gold-hungry Rogue, an unkillable Juggernaut, or a life-stealing Vampiric Mage. Earn talent points by leveling up, spend gold to unlock them.

Master 21 spells. From classic Fireball and Ice Shard to Meteor, Chain Lightning and Whirlwind — every ability is unlocked through talents, with cooldowns, mana costs and status effects like burn, poison, freeze and curse. Two rare spellbooks can only be found through crafting.

Craft, loot, and trade. Collect materials from chests and fallen enemies, forge 17 recipes — weapons, armor, potions and spellbooks — then visit the Shop to buy gear or sell spare materials. Equip swords, staffs and armor that change your stats.

Four difficulty modes. Normal for a relaxed crawl, Medium for a challenge, Hardcore where death wipes all your saves, and Fog mode that limits your vision to 4 tiles — perfect for tense, atmospheric runs.

Features: fog of war with smooth field-of-view, minimap, AI enemies that hunt and chase you, ranged archers and mages, F5/F9 quick-saving, and a fully resizable window.
